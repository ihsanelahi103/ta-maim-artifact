## Why this study (diagnosis of the earlier null results, stated before running)
1. Not a learning problem. For budgeted verification the best action is a ranking by posterior risk. The learner (TA-DDQN-V) reached the level of the best rule (within 0.2% in quality-matching, binding B=1) but cannot exceed information the rules already have.
2. The +5% net-utility criterion was nearly unreachable by construction: honest value (~10,700) dominates; even a perfect-label oracle improves the best rule by only 6-10% of U (headroom .067/.100/.058), and the remaining regret is mostly poison before any evidence exists (seed-7 training stream, SUS, B=1: 75 of 89 total poison in qmatch and 33 of 33 in collude occur in episodes 1-6, i.e. before the CATS latch; in weak-signal band1/band2 poison accumulates until episodes 40-80). A noisy-verifier policy cannot recover the ORACLE ceiling. The criterion therefore measured almost nothing about allocation quality.
3. The comparators' fixed orderings waste checks (SUS/FLAG rank high suspicion first, which includes Sybils already dropped by the latch). This is a hypothesis, not a result; this study tests whether a utility-derived index does better.

## Policy (no free parameters; no learning of the allocation)
VOI index: for each unresolved eligible contributor j at episode ep: posterior Sybil logit = prior logit + running LLR (prior = logistic regression on the 7 features [1,S,C,suspicion,latch,window mean,5xwindow SD], fitted on pre-training streams of the OTHER families with labels, like TUNED-LIN); one check moves the LLR by -d*v (Sybil -> N(L+d^2/2, d^2); honest -> N(L-d^2/2, d^2)); a confirmation (|LLR|>ln 9) is permanent. Default decision (shared): merge iff not latched. index = R * [ p * P(cross +A | Sybil) * lam * q * (not latched) + (1-p) * P(cross -A | honest) * q * latched ] - c, R = (E-ep) * (past mining rate), q = window-15 mean quality. Top-B by index per episode. Design-time checks were execution-only (60-episode truncations of seed-7 streams, test=train; no number used); nothing was tuned.

## Budgets, comparators, metrics
Primary budget B=1 (the only budget binding under the earlier pre-registered definition; if B=1 is not binding on the new streams by the same definition the study is uninformative). B=2 secondary (reported, non-binding by that definition). c=.10, d'=2, lam=1, status rule +-ln 9, verifier and utility exactly as in lm2/lm3. Comparator = strongest of {RANDOM, RR, SUS, FLAG, BND, UNC, TUNED-LIN} by mean net utility on the held-out family (chosen with test information: conservative). Metrics reported separately: net utility, regret (= ORACLE - U per stream; ORACLE = free perfect labels, an unattainable ceiling), poison merged, verification cost, honest-dropped share, Sybil-merged share.

## Success criterion (primary)
Per family: mean per-stream relative regret reduction (regret_rule - regret_VOI)/regret_rule >= 0.10, paired bootstrap 95% CI (2000 draws over streams) lower bound > 0, honest-dropped and Sybil-merged shares each <= the rule's + 0.02. PASS overall = criterion met in >= 2 of 3 families at B=1. NO-HEADROOM if the strongest rule's regret < 2% of its utility. INCONCLUSIVE if a family CI contains 0 and 0.10. FAIL otherwise. The old criterion (+5% net utility) is printed for transparency and is expected not to be met. The metric change is made after seeing the B=1 results on seeds 101-105 and is motivated by the diagnosis above; the new seeds are untouched. All outcomes, including failures, are reported; no retuning after test results.

## What a PASS or FAIL allows
PASS: at most one appendix sentence (offline replay, modelled verifier, regret reduction x% relative to the best fixed rule on independent streams); no TA-DDQN claim; not a contribution. FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE: no change to the manuscript beyond (optionally) one clause that a utility-derived index also gives no gain. Expectation stated in advance: a modest regret reduction is plausible; a large one is not; the early-evidence floor limits any policy.

## Limits
Offline replay of exogenous streams; modelled verifier with fixed d'; labels used at fit time on training streams (prior, TUNED-LIN); the myopic one-check VOI ignores multi-check plans; the oracle is not attainable.
